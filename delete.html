You can permanently delete your Erik's Army account and associated data at any time, using either method below.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Erik's Army and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.
Your account and data are removed right away.

Option 2 — By request (no app needed)

Email jeffrey@stellarideasllc.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account." We'll verify it's you and delete your account within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What gets deleted

Your account is permanently removed, along with the personal data tied to it: your email and username, profile photo, subscription/purchase status, notification token, and app activity. Your chat messages are deleted or irreversibly anonymized so they can no longer be linked to you.

What we may keep

We may retain a limited amount of information where required by law, to resolve disputes, or to prevent abuse (for example, basic transaction records the app stores keep, or records of policy violations). Anything retained is kept only as long as necessary and is not used to re-create your account.
