Common questions

How do I report or block someone?

Long-press any message to report it (with a reason) or block its author. Blocked users' messages are hidden from you. Manage blocks in Profile → Blocked accounts.

How do I manage or cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel anytime in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Use "Restore purchases" on the Premium screen if a purchase isn't showing.

How do I delete my account?

In the App, go to Profile to delete your account and data, or email us and we'll remove it.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check Profile → Notifications in the App, and make sure notifications are allowed for Erik's Army in iOS Settings.

Why don't I see matches for some leagues?

Outside their season, leagues have no fixtures. During the summer international tournament and active league play you'll see the day's matches.
